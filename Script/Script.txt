What if India could track every drone flying through its airspace?

The Indian Air Force has demonstrated a new indigenous system called... **Vayu UTtaM.**

It was showcased during **Dronathon 2026**, at Pokhran, Rajasthan.

So... what does it actually do?

Vayu UTtaM is an **Unmanned Traffic Management system**, designed to detect and track both **military and civilian drones**, in real time.

And here's where it gets interesting.

As the number of drones in the sky keeps increasing, it becomes harder to know which drone is authorised...

and which one could be a potential threat.

Vayu UTtaM is designed to help manage this increasingly crowded airspace, and support the identification of **unauthorised or rogue drones**.

The system could potentially have applications beyond the military too...

including **civil aviation, critical infrastructure, and public safety.**

And this is just one of the technologies India showcased at **Dronathon 2026.**

The future of airspace may not just be about fighter jets...

but also about **who controls the drones in the sky.**
