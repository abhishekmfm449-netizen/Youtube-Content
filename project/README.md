# Vayu UTtaM — YouTube Short

A 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 Short about the Indian Air Force's Vayu UTtaM unmanned
traffic management system, cut to the supplied voiceover.

**Output:** `out/Vayu_UTtaM_YouTube_Short.mp4` (82.6 s)
Alongside it: `out/Vayu_UTtaM_YouTube_Short.srt`, `out/thumbnail.jpg` and
`out/asset_manifest.json` (every crop window, every exclusion, machine-readable).
Scene timings land in `build/scenes.json`.

Everything is generated by the scripts in `src/`. Re-running them reproduces the
video exactly — see [How to rebuild](#how-to-rebuild).

---

## What the video is built from

| Layer | Source |
|---|---|
| Narration | The supplied ElevenLabs read — used verbatim, untouched, and it drives every cut |
| Photographic content | The supplied images and clips in `Image/` and `Video/` |
| Maps, radar, tracks, HUD, titles, captions | Rendered from code in `src/` |
| Music and sound effects | Synthesised from scratch in `src/sound.py` |

No stock footage was downloaded and no new AI imagery was generated. The only
external files fetched are six open-licence fonts and one public map boundary
file, both listed under [Third-party assets](#third-party-assets).

## Editorial decisions you should know about

### The voiceover is the master timeline

The supplied read is 80.56 s of speech; the video is 82.6 s with a 1.8 s held end
card. The narration is **not** stretched, compressed or re-timed — it is
peak-normalised and otherwise untouched.

Line boundaries were read off the recording's own pause map and checked line by
line against the script, then pinned in
`assets/voiceover/alignment.json`, keyed on a SHA-256 of the audio.

`src/voiceover.py` also contains an automatic aligner (a dynamic-programming
assignment of speech segments to script lines) which runs for any voiceover that
does not match the pin. It gets 12 of these 14 lines right on its own; the two it
misses are a breath-length pause between "…in real time." and "And here's where
it gets interesting.", so the shipped alignment is the checked one rather than the
computed one. **If you replace the voiceover, the pin stops applying, the
automatic aligner takes over, and you should check `build/timeline.json` before
rendering.**

### Supplied images I could not use as they stand

The brief rules out fabricated official interfaces, fake insignia, fake
government branding and text baked into AI images. Several supplied images
contain exactly those things. Rather than throw whole images away, each one
declares the rectangle that is actually usable, in `src/media.py`, and only that
rectangle reaches the screen.

**Dropped entirely (2 of 10 images):**

| File | Why |
|---|---|
| `ChatGPT Image Sep 22, 2026, 08_33_08 PM.png` | Fabricates an Indian Air Force crest and banner, plus an official-looking "Vayu UTtaM" system display |
| `ChatGPT Image Sep 22, 2026, 08_26_47 PM.png` | Tricolour shield emblem, "Indian Defence Ecosystem" lockup, soldier silhouetted against a flag — a quasi-official emblem and a propaganda register the brief asks to avoid |

**Used, but cropped to exclude a forbidden region:**

| File | Excluded region |
|---|---|
| `ChatGPT Image Sep 22, 2026, 08_22_17 PM.png` | Airframe carrying a fabricated "INDIAN ARMED FORCES" marking and flag decal, and a system graphic with "VAYU UTTAM" baked in. Only the bare rotor is used. |
| `Gemini_Generated_Image_1ugihz…png` | A "Government of India / National Airspace Monitoring" branded interface, and the prompt text the generator printed into the render |
| `Gemini_Generated_Image_22a726…png` | The printed prompt text, and a large tricolour shield emblem |
| `Gemini_Generated_Image_laxniv…png` | The printed prompt text |
| `ChatGPT Image Sep 22, 2026, 08_25_42 PM.png` | Icon rails and an inset map, cropped for framing rather than for content |

Three things I used but want to flag, because they are judgement calls rather
than clear-cut:

- The supplied console clip has **"Vayu UTtaM" rendered on a screen inside the
  footage**. It is stylised rather than presented as a real screenshot, and it is
  your asset, so I kept it — but it is a depicted system display, not a real one.
- The craft in `Gemini_Generated_Image_22a726…png` carry **tricolour roundel-like
  markings**. They are small and decorative at final scale, and the scene is
  short, but they are invented markings.
- The supplied stills carry baked HUD text (target IDs, speeds, altitudes). None
  of it is presented as official, but it was generated, not measured.

Every scene built on an interface-like plate carries a standing
**"ILLUSTRATIVE VISUALISATION — NOT AN ACTUAL SYSTEM DISPLAY"** line, so nothing
in the video reads as operational footage.

### The India map

The outline is the **official Indian boundary**, from DataMeet's
`india-composite.geojson` (CC BY 4.0). The far more common Natural Earth country
layer — which ships inside GeoPandas and would have been the easy choice — draws
Jammu & Kashmir on de-facto control lines, which is not how India's map should be
depicted. The DataMeet composite runs to 37.1° N and includes the full claimed
territory, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep.

The projection is equirectangular with a cos(latitude) correction at India's mid
latitude, so the outline keeps its true proportions and is never stretched to fit
a frame.

### Claims

The video says only what the script says. In particular it does **not** claim
that Vayu UTtaM tracks every drone in India, that it destroys rogue drones, or
that it gives complete control of Indian airspace. Scene 2 carries an explicit
"DEMONSTRATED — NOT YET IN SERVICE" line. Scene 8 is about *identification*: no
weapon, no intercept, no explosion anywhere in the video.

### Captions

Burned in, three to six words per card, word-by-word reveal timed by syllable
weight within each line. Key phrases from the script's own bold markers pick up
the system accent colour. The band from 65 % to 82 % of frame height is reserved
for them — no scene graphic is allowed into it, and nothing meaningful sits below
83 %, where YouTube's own chrome covers the frame.

## How to rebuild

```bash
cd project
pip install pillow numpy                       # and geopandas+shapely only for prep_geo.py
python3 src/prep_media.py                      # extract the clip frame cache (once, ~90 MB)
python3 src/voiceover.py                       # narration.wav + timeline.json
python3 src/render.py                          # the film
python3 src/thumbnail.py
python3 src/qc.py                              # 20 automated checks on the result
```

Or just `./build.sh`, which runs all of the above.

`render.py` takes `--preview` (half resolution, fast), `--range A:B` (seconds, for
checking one scene) and `--jobs N`. A full render is about 25 CPU-minutes.

`src/prep_geo.py` regenerates `assets/geo/india_outline.json` from the DataMeet
source and needs network access; the simplified outline is committed, so a normal
rebuild does not.

### Replacing the voiceover

Drop a new file into `Voiceover/` (or `project/assets/voiceover/`) and re-run
`voiceover.py` then `render.py`. Every scene's timing, the captions and the SRT
are functions of the line boundaries, so the whole edit re-times itself. Check
`build/timeline.json` first — see the note on alignment above.

If no voiceover is present at all, `voiceover.py` falls back to synthesising a
scratch narration with Kokoro (Apache-2.0) so the edit can still be previewed.
That path needs the model files and is not used for this build.

## Layout of the project

```
project/
  src/
    script.py      the narration text, scene map and caption emphasis list
    voiceover.py   narration -> narration.wav + timeline.json (the master timeline)
    media.py       supplied footage: crop windows, the grade, what was excluded
    prep_media.py  one-off extraction of the clip frame cache
    prep_geo.py    one-off simplification of the India boundary
    design.py      palette, type ramp, drawing primitives, the map projection
    elements.py    reusable elements: tracks, drones, HUD chrome, terrain
    scenes.py      the fourteen scenes
    captions.py    caption cards, word timing, SRT
    sound.py       synthesised music bed and sound effects
    render.py      scene spans, frame render, audio mix, encode
  assets/
    fonts/         six open-licence fonts (see below)
    geo/           the simplified India outline
    voiceover/     the pinned line alignment; also where a replacement VO can go
  build/           intermediates: narration.wav, timeline.json, scenes.json, cache/
  out/             mp4, srt, thumbnail, asset_manifest.json
  build.sh         runs the whole chain end to end
```

## Technical output

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 High @ 4.1, CRF 17, `yuv420p`, `+faststart`
- AAC 192 kbit/s, 48 kHz stereo
- Narration peaks at −1.0 dBFS; bed and effects sit about 13 dB under it, with
  4 dB of side-chain ducking under speech; no clipped samples
- Every frame is composed at 1080×1920 and all camera moves scale ≥ 1.0, so no
  black bars or pillarboxing can occur; the 16:9 FPV clip is centre-cropped to
  9:16 rather than letterboxed
